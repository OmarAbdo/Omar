CourseNow

CourseNow is a small Windows desktop app I wrote for my own studies at the Europa-Universität Viadrina. It reads the university's course catalogue and my own enrolments, and puts every meeting and deadline of my courses into one calendar.

What it does

  • Searches the course catalogue and shows how each course takes registrations.
  • Builds a calendar of my courses: every meeting, every deadline, and which ones are compulsory or clash.
  • Optionally copies that calendar to Google Calendar, so it is on my phone with reminders.

How it uses Google Calendar

When connected, CourseNow creates one calendar named “CourseNow” in the signed-in Google account and keeps the events in it up to date. It asks Google only for permission to manage calendars it created itself. It cannot see or change any other calendar, and it reads nothing else from the Google account.

The app runs entirely on the user's own computer. There is no CourseNow server and no account to create. The details are in the privacy policy.

Availability

CourseNow is a personal project. It is not a product, it is not sold, and it is not offered to the public.
